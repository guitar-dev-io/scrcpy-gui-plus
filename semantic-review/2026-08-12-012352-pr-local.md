JPEG after a valid hello, or a Tauri channel send failure. No status is emitted for those cases; the listener continues accepting until the 120-second setup deadline or eight counted failures.

The React listener only clears the preview and streaming flags for `stopped`, `disconnected`, or `error` events (`src/hooks/useCompanion.ts:118-143`). Because the post-authentication failure emits none of them, `screenFrame` keeps the last blob URL and `isScreenStreaming` remains true. The UI then offers Stop rather than a fresh Start, and the screen generation is not advanced to invalidate late frames. Track whether authentication completed, emit a terminal screen status and invalidate that generation when the authenticated stream ends, and let React clear the frame and enable restart; keep silent bounded retry only for clients rejected before authentication.
